STPhrases_GeneratedFromRegionalPhrases.txt is not in the OpenCC repository:
OpenCC's data/CMakeLists.txt generates it at build time from HKPhrases.txt and
TWPhrases.txt (simplified projections of their keys through t2s.json), and
s2t.json and s2tw.json use it next to STPhrases.txt. It was regenerated from
the commit above, following that rule, with

    bash scripts/fetch-opencc.sh
    python3 -I scripts/opencc_regional_phrases.py \
        ~/Library/Caches/FontsMaker/opencc-025f371dc76b598d77384fbdab90c937471844d8 <work dir> <output>

(Homebrew opencc 1.4.2 and opencc_dict as tools). Its 556 entries equal the
STPhrases_GeneratedFromRegionalPhrases.ocd2 that Homebrew's opencc 1.4.2
installs (dumped with opencc_dict); scripts/verify-s2t-phrases.sh repeats both
the regeneration and the comparison.
